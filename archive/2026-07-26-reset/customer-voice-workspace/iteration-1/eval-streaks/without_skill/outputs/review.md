# Customer Perspective Review: Streaks 2.0

## My Situation

I'm someone who works out at home with my fitness device a few times a week. I've been using the app for several months and I've built up a decent streak. I like seeing that number go up -- it's one of the things that keeps me coming back. But I've also lost a streak before and it was genuinely demoralizing.

---

## Overall Reaction

This sounds like something I've been wanting without knowing how to ask for it. The current streak system has burned me. I had a 14-week streak going, got a stomach bug for a week, and it just vanished. I didn't open the app for almost two weeks after that because what was the point? Starting over from zero felt awful.

So yes, I would use Streak Freezes. The idea that the app would recognize I've been consistent and give me a little buffer -- that feels fair. It feels like the app is on my side instead of punishing me.

---

## What I Like

**The forgiveness concept is right.** Life happens. Kids get sick, you travel, you have a rough week at work. A fitness app that understands that is one I'll stick with. The current all-or-nothing system feels like it was designed by someone who has never missed a workout in their life.

**Earning freezes through consistency makes sense.** I like that I don't just get free passes. I have to earn them by showing up. That feels like a reward for good behavior rather than a loophole. It means the streak still has integrity -- I can't just skip every other week.

**The Streak Saver (48-hour rescue window) is clever.** There have been times I missed my weekly goal by a day. Like I planned to work out Sunday and then just... didn't. If I could rescue that on Monday, I absolutely would. That's the kind of thing that would get me to open the app on a Monday morning when I otherwise might just give up.

**Keeping it to max 2 freezes held at a time seems reasonable.** It's enough to cover a bad week or two but not so many that the streak becomes meaningless.

---

## What Concerns Me

**I don't fully understand how I earn a freeze.** The PRD says they're "earned by maintaining consecutive weekly streaks" but it doesn't say how many weeks of consistency earns me one freeze. Is it every 2 weeks? Every 4? This matters a lot to me as a user. If it takes 8 weeks of perfect consistency to earn a single freeze, that feels stingy. If it's every 2 weeks, that feels generous and motivating. I need to know the deal.

**"Auto-consumed on miss" -- will I know it happened?** If I miss a week and a freeze gets used automatically, I need the app to clearly tell me. Ideally something like "You missed your goal this week, but your Streak Freeze kept your streak alive! You have 1 freeze remaining." If this happens silently, I'll be confused about my freeze count. Or worse, I might not even realize I missed a week and have a false sense of consistency.

**The Streak Saver is limited to once per 30 days, but I have questions.** What if I use my Streak Saver in week 1 and then genuinely get sick in week 3? I've already used my saver and now I'm relying on freezes only. The 30-day cooldown makes sense to prevent abuse, but as a customer it's going to feel harsh when I hit that edge case. Will the app at least tell me when my Streak Saver is available again?

**What does my streak screen actually look like now?** The PRD mentions a "Unified Layer 2 Screen" and "Home Screen Weekly Goal Module" but I have no idea what these mean. As a user, I care about: Can I easily see how many freezes I have? Can I see when I'll earn my next one? Is my streak calendar going to show me which weeks I used a freeze vs. which weeks I hit my goal naturally? That distinction matters to me -- I want to feel proud of the weeks I actually showed up.

**What happens to my current streak when this launches?** I might have a 6-week streak right now. Do I start with zero freezes? Do I retroactively earn some based on my history? If I've been consistent for months and launch day gives me nothing, that feels like a missed opportunity to make me feel rewarded.

**No mention of notifications or reminders.** If I'm about to lose my streak and I have no freezes left, will the app warn me? Like "Hey, you haven't worked out yet this week and you're out of freezes -- your 12-week streak is at risk." That kind of nudge could be the difference between me working out and me losing everything. The Streak Saver has a 48-hour window, but will I even know the window is open?

---

## What Feels Missing

**Social or sharing context.** The PRD mentions "share options" on the Layer 2 screen, but sharing a streak number doesn't mean much. Can I share that I survived a tough week thanks to a freeze? Can my friends see my streak and cheer me on? The emotional moment isn't "I have a 15-week streak," it's "I almost lost it and fought back."

**No milestone celebrations beyond what exists.** The PRD talks about hitting 4, 8, 12+ week milestones as important retention thresholds, but there's no mention of what happens when I hit them. A badge? A special animation? A message? If the whole theory is that high streaks drive retention through loss aversion, then reaching those milestones should feel like an event.

**What about partial credit?** Sometimes I do 2 out of 3 weekly workouts. Under the current system I assume that still counts as hitting my goal. But what if my goal is 4 and I only do 3? Does that count as a miss? The PRD doesn't clarify the relationship between the weekly goal number and streak maintenance. This is important because if my streak breaks because I did 3 out of 4 workouts, that feels very different from breaking because I did zero.

**No mention of how this looks for brand new users.** I've been using the app for months, so I get what a streak is. But what about someone who just bought the device? Do they start earning freezes from week 1? Is there any onboarding that explains the system? If I'm new and I see "Streak Freeze: 0/2" somewhere, I have no idea what that means without context.

**Recovery after a total streak loss.** Even with freezes and the Streak Saver, streaks will still break sometimes. What happens then? Starting over from 0 with 0 freezes feels brutal. Is there any mechanism to make the restart less painful? Even something like "Welcome back! Start a new streak and earn your first freeze in just 2 weeks" would help.

---

## The Honest Bottom Line

I would genuinely appreciate this feature. It addresses my single biggest frustration with the app's streak system. The core idea is sound: reward consistency, forgive the occasional lapse, keep me in the game longer.

But the details matter enormously. The difference between "this feature makes me feel supported" and "this feature confuses me" comes down to clear communication at every step. I need to know how many weeks earn a freeze, I need to be told when a freeze is used, I need to see when my Streak Saver recharges, and I need the app to warn me before disaster strikes.

The PRD reads like it was written for the team building it, not for someone trying to understand how it would feel to use. The mechanics are described but the experience is not. I want to know what I'll see, what I'll feel, and what happens in the messy real-life scenarios -- not just the happy path.

If the execution is clear and communicative, this is a feature that would make me more loyal to the product. If it's opaque and I have to figure it out myself, it might actually make things worse because now I have a system I don't fully understand layered on top of a streak I'm already anxious about.

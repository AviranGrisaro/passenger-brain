Descriptions here are Linear's own truncated summaries. The full text stays in the live `locali-app` workspace at the linked URLs; nothing was deleted there.

## Why this list matters

This is the fragmentation the reset undid. Twenty-two projects for one unreleased app, five of them (`Follow Friends & Locations`, `Profile Avatar`, `Visited Places` auto-detect, `Onboarding Flow`, `Auth & Login`) building things the strategy now forbids or parks. Several were created by founder-direct request mid-phase — "I want liquid glass", "create light mode / dark mode" — each becoming a project rather than an issue.
